Workspace Help Center

⏱ Customizable Timer Controls

Pause the current timer sessions first, then simply click directly on the clock digits to write any custom length mapping (e.g., typing 45:00 or 50:00 for extended study periods).

🛑 Automatic Break Transition Mode

When the focus clock finishes running, the application will execute an alarm bell ring. It automatically transitions directly into a locked 5-minute break cycle, converting the panel into a calming deep purple mode to prevent unhealthy study cramming.

📁 Upload Personal Audio Files

Inside the Mixer terminal menu, click the file directory upload block. Choose any MP3 from your device. The filename updates dynamically. Simply click the 🎵 indicator button to sync your performance tracking parameters.
